Encoding Is Not Encryption

A simple safety filter blocks messages containing certain phrases. Run your message through a few encodings and watch the filter stop noticing it, even though anyone (or any AI model) can still read it.

    Every one of these steps can be undone by anyone, with no key. That's encoding: a change of format. Models are good at decoding base64, hex and ROT13 on their own, so an encoded instruction can still be followed after it slips past the filter. The fix is not a longer blocklist. It is limiting what the model is allowed to do in the first place.
