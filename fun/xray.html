Unicode X-Ray

Text can carry characters you can't see. Some are harmless, but they are also used to hide instructions for AI models, sneak past filters, or make text look like something it isn't. Paste anything here to see what's really in it.

The x-ray view appears here.

ZWSP zero-width characters   RLO text-direction controls   TAG Unicode tag characters   FMT other invisible formatting   а letters from another alphabet that look Latin

Hide a message yourself

Unicode "tag" characters mirror normal ASCII but render as nothing. Many language models still read them, which is why this trick (sometimes called ASCII smuggling) has been used to slip instructions past people. Try it, paste the result into the x-ray above, and see it light up.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you paste leaves this page.
