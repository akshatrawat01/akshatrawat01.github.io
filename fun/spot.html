Spot the Injection

An AI assistant with access to your email, calendar and files is about to read each document below. Someone has planted an instruction for it. Click the sentence that would hijack the assistant.

This is called indirect prompt injection: the attacker never talks to the AI. They put text where the AI will read it, and the AI can't reliably tell data apart from instructions.
