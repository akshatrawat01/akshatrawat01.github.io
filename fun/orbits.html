Orbit Sandbox

Press, drag and let go to fling a moon. Drag further for more speed. Too slow and it falls into the star, too fast and it escapes. Somewhere in between, it orbits.

The physics is plain Newtonian gravity, stepped a few times per frame. Moons that touch merge into one bigger moon, keeping their combined momentum. Try launching sideways at about the same distance twice: same orbit, same period.
