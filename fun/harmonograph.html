Pendulum Drawing Machine

A harmonograph is a Victorian drawing toy: two pendulums swing a pen and a table at the same time, and friction slowly calms them down. Small changes in their rhythm give completely different drawings.

Tip: speeds close to simple ratios like 2 : 3 or 3 : 4 make tidy, symmetric shapes. Nudge one slightly off and the drawing slowly rotates into a spiral.
