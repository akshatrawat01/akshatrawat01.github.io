About me

I'm an MSc student in Information Security at Stockholm University. I'm interested in how systems fail under pressure, and lately most of that curiosity goes into AI security: how language models and agents can be steered into doing things they shouldn't, and how to defend against it.

Before my master's I interned with the Madhya Pradesh State Cyber Police in Bhopal, writing OSINT reports for cybercrime investigations, and then spent two years as a Cyber Security Engineer at Inventico Apps working on access control and identity management. Right now I'm a teaching assistant for Introduction to Information Security at Stockholm University, where I design and build the hands-on labs students work through.
