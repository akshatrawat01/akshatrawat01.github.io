About me

I'm an MSc student in Information Security at Stockholm University. I'm interested in how systems fail under pressure, and lately most of that curiosity goes into AI security: how language models and agents can be steered into doing things they shouldn't, and how to defend against it.

Before my master's I interned with the Madhya Pradesh State Cyber Police in Bhopal, writing OSINT reports for cybercrime investigations, and then spent two years as a Cyber Security Engineer at Inventico Apps working on access control and identity management. Right now I'm a teaching assistant for Introduction to Information Security at Stockholm University, where I design and build the hands-on labs students work through.

If you like poking at things, the Fun page has a few small AI security games that run in your browser.

Projects

  • Sentinel Home Lab: an isolated Azure lab with Microsoft Sentinel and a Windows honeypot that catches 900+ RDP brute-force attempts a day, plus PowerShell that parses and enriches the logs. 2025
  • Tatou: a live attack and defend exercise for Software Security. We hardened a Dockerized web platform while attacking other groups, and captured flags from four groups in the first phase. 2026
  • Android forensics case: a formal investigation report for Digital Forensics, with chain of custody, a reconstructed timeline and every finding tied to evidence. 2025
  • Enigma Machine Simulation: the WWII cipher machine rebuilt in code, with rotors, reflector and plugboard.
